### 5. Actionable Recommendations  

| Recommendation | Rationale | Suggested Technique |
|----------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Investigate zero discount entries** (1,740 zeros) | May represent data entry errors, free‑item promotions, or placeholder values that distort summary statistics. | Flag as “questionable”; consider imputing with the variable’s median (10) or removing if deemed erroneous. |
| **Apply log‑transformation** to **Unit_Price** and **Total_Amount** | Both variables exhibit extreme skewness (skew > 3) and very high kurtosis, inflating mean and variance. | `log1p(x)` or `log(x)` prior to analysis; re‑evaluate descriptive stats and outlier bounds. |
| **Winsorize or cap extreme values** in **Unit_Price** (max ≈ 309 k) and **Total_Amount** (max ≈ 1.2 M) | Outliers represent a small % of rows but dominate dispersion metrics, biasing models. | Set upper caps at, e.g., the 99th percentile (≈ 65 k for Unit_Price, ≈ 150 k for Total_Amount) or use robust scaling (e.g., robust Z‑score). |
| **Review low‑cardinality variables** (Quantity, Discount) | Their limited unique values suggest they may be better treated as categorical or binned variables. | Encode as factors or create bins (e.g., quantity = {0‑1, 2‑3, >3}) to stabilize variance. |
| **Standardize/normalize** after transformation | To bring all variables onto comparable scales for modeling (e.g., regression, clustering). | Use robust scaling (median & IQR) after log‑transform to mitigate remaining skew. |
| **Document data‑quality notes** for stakeholders | Zero‑discount proportion (11.6 %) and high cardinality of price/amount need explanation (e.g., promotional pricing, separate price tiers). | Add metadata notes; consider creating a “discount_flag” variable to capture zero‑discount cases explicitly. |
| **Re‑evaluate modeling assumptions** | Normality tests are all significant; parametric models may be inappropriate. | Prefer tree‑based models, robust regression, or non‑parametric methods; if parametric, use bootstrapped confidence intervals. |

**Bottom Line**  
The dataset is complete but heavily skewed, with pronounced outliers in monetary variables, a non‑trivial proportion of zero discounts, and a mix of low‑ and high‑cardinality features. Transforming the highly skewed monetary fields (log‑scale) and applying outlier capping/winsorization will reduce distortion, improve model performance, and yield more reliable insights. Zero‑discount values should be examined and either cleaned or explicitly flagged. All other variables are relatively clean, with acceptable missing‑value and negative‑value records.
